# CS 250: Software Development Lifecycle

## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program?

I interpret user needs by first understanding what the user wants the program to accomplish and what problems they need it to solve. User stories help me break these needs into smaller, manageable requirements that guide the development process. They also help me focus on the user's perspective instead of only thinking about the technical side of the program. By reviewing requirements and testing the software, I can make sure the final product meets the user's expectations.

## How do I approach developing programs?

I approach software development by breaking a project into smaller tasks and completing them step by step. Throughout this course, I learned how Agile and Scrum practices help developers organize their work, communicate with team members, and make improvements throughout development. In my future development work, I hope to use sprints, sprint reviews, and retrospectives to track progress, identify problems, and learn from each stage of a project. I also want to use feedback and regular testing to improve the quality of my software.

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member means communicating effectively, respecting other people's ideas, completing assigned tasks, and taking responsibility for my work. Software development often requires people with different skills to collaborate toward a shared goal. A good team member should be willing to ask questions, help others solve problems, accept constructive feedback, and adapt when requirements change. I believe these qualities help a team work more efficiently and produce better software.
